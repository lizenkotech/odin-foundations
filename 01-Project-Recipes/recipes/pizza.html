Veggie Pizza

Prep Time: 25 mins

Cook Time: 10 mins

Additional Time: 1 hr 5 mins

Total Time: 1 hr 40 mins

Servings:16

Yield:1 pizza

Nutrition (per serving)

196 Calories

13g Fat

16g Carbs

5g Protein

Ingredients

🥖 Base

  • 2 (8 ounce) packages refrigerated crescent rolls

🥗 Vegetables

  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 1 stalk celery, thinly sliced
  • 1 ½ cups chopped fresh broccoli
  • ½ cup radishes, sliced

🧀 Sauce

  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 (1 ounce) package ranch seasoning mix
  • 1 teaspoon dried dill weed
  • ¼ teaspoon garlic salt

Directions

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Press crescent roll dough into a baking pan to form a crust. Let stand 5 minutes and pierce with a fork.
  3. Bake until golden brown, about 10 minutes. Let cool completely.
  4. Mix sour cream, cream cheese, ranch seasoning, dill, and garlic salt.
  5. Spread mixture over cooled crust.
  6. Top with vegetables evenly.
  7. Chill for 1–2 hours, then cut into squares and serve.
