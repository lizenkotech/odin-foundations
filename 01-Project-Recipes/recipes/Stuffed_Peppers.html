Stuffed Peppers with Turkey and Vegetables

Prep: 20 mins

Cook: 40 mins

Total: 1 hr

Servings: 4


Nutrition (per serving)

Calories: 279

Fat: 16g

Carbs: 10g

Protein: 25g

Ingredients:

🌶️ Vegetables

  • 4 green bell peppers, tops removed, seeded
  • ½ onion, chopped
  • 1 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 1 zucchini, chopped
  • ½ red bell pepper, chopped
  • ½ yellow bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 cup fresh spinach

🍖 Protein

  • 1 pound ground turkey

🧄 Seasoning & Extras

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 can diced tomatoes, drained
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • Italian seasoning to taste
  • garlic powder to taste
  • salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Gather all ingredients.
  3. Wrap green bell peppers in foil and bake for 15 minutes.
  4. Cook turkey in a skillet until browned. Set aside.
  5. Sauté onion, mushrooms, zucchini, peppers, and spinach in oil until tender.
  6. Return turkey to skillet, add tomatoes and tomato paste, season well.
  7. Stuff peppers with mixture and return to oven for 15 minutes.
  8. Serve hot and enjoy!
