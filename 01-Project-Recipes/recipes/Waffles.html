Classic Waffles

Prep Time: 10 mins

Cook Time: 15 mins

Total Time: 25 mins

Servings: 5

Yield: 10 waffles

Nutrition (per serving)

Calories: 310

Fat: 14g

Carbs: 40g

Protein: 8g

Ingredients

🥞 Batter

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 4 teaspoons baking powder
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 ½ cups milk
  • ⅓ cup butter, melted
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Directions

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Preheat your waffle iron.
  3. Mix flour, salt, baking powder, and sugar in a bowl.
  4. In another bowl, beat eggs, then add milk, butter, and vanilla.
  5. Combine wet and dry ingredients until smooth.
  6. Pour batter into waffle iron.
  7. Cook until golden and crisp.
  8. Serve immediately and enjoy!
